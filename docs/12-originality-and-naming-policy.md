# 12 — Originality and naming policy

SAND-1024 must become a genuinely custom architecture, not an existing ISA with renamed instructions.

Independently specify register organisation, block semantics, instruction encodings, arithmetic edge cases, control flow, memory translation, privilege transitions, exception and interrupt handling, reset, boot contract, executable format, and ABI.

Existing architectures may be studied to understand trade-offs. A materially derived idea should be acknowledged and evaluated. Do not copy proprietary code, text, diagrams, or specifications.

Orryxen, Mon, Monolith Array, Axiom, Axiom Path, Nexum Core, Deepframe, and Axiom Forge are provisional project terms. Their use does not establish global uniqueness or trademark rights.

Originality is demonstrated by the specification, machine-readable ISA, emulator, tests, assembler, and ABI—not by terminology alone.
